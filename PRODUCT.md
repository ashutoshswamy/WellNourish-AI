# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
General health-minded adults with a goal (lose, gain or maintain weight), a diet preference and sometimes allergies, who want a ready plan for the week without paying a dietitian.

## Product Purpose
WellNourish AI turns a short profile (age, sex, weight, height, goal weight, activity, diet, allergies) into a 7-day meal plan with calorie and macro targets per day and per meal, plus an auto-generated grocery list. Success: a signed-in user generates a plan, cooks from it, and ticks off the grocery list.

## Positioning
The whole week and the shopping list arrive together, free, from one profile: plan, macros and groceries in one go.

## Operating Context
Plans are generated by Google Gemini with structured JSON output. Accounts via Firebase Auth (email/password or Google) with server-verified session cookies. One new plan per user per hour. Routes: / (landing), /sign-in, /sign-up, /dashboard, /plan, /history, /shopping-list, /profile, /privacy, /terms, /cookies. Deployed on Vercel at wellnourishai.ashutoshswamy.in.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Next.js 16 App Router, React 19, Tailwind v4, react-hook-form + zod, lucide-react icons.
- Plans: 7 days, meals with protein/carbs/fat, grocery list with check-off, history sortable and deletable.
- Light and dark themes follow the system with a manual toggle; both must survive.
- Routes, API contracts, form field names and SEO metadata stay stable.
- Toasts: sileo library, site-wide.

## Brand Commitments
Name "WellNourish AI". Visual identity is open for replacement (user chose a new visual world); logo may change.

## Evidence on Hand
No testimonials, customer logos, user counts, ratings or press exist. Do not fabricate any. Real assets: public/og-image.jpg, logo.png, app icons. Demonstration meal data may be authored but labelled as sample.

## Product Principles
- Show the deliverable (a real week of meals, macros, groceries) rather than describe it.
- Constraints (allergies, diet) are first-class, never fine print.
- Numbers are exact and legible; nothing decorative competes with them.
- Honest: no invented proof, free stays free.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
WCAG AA contrast in both themes, keyboard focus, reduced-motion respected.
